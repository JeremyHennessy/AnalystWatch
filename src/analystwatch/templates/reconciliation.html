SAFETY MODEL

Reconciliation resolves evidence; it does not guess

Prepared

The attempt was claimed but does not have a definitive recorded outcome. Retry remains blocked.

Confirm succeeded

Use only when durable external evidence shows the provider accepted or completed the delivery.

Confirm failed

Use only when evidence shows the provider did not accept or complete the delivery. Existing retry policy then determines when another attempt is allowed.
